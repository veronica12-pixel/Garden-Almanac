Sow and transplant dates start from what worked in this Odessa, FL (Zone 9B) garden last year, then align to the nearest matching Fruit / Leaf / Root / Flower day on the Biodynamic Calendar tab — some windows span a few days (e.g. "Aug 12-14"), and that tab opens on whichever month is current.

Tap a crop card to expand it. Cards sort by whichever date is coming up soonest, and flag Overdue if a planned date's passed with nothing logged yet. Missing a crop? Use + Add on any tab; the ✕ next to Season DONE removes one for the season.

Log what actually happened in Actual sow / Actual transplant. Every crop lets you tag a variety as F1 or OP (open-pollinated) as you type it in; for tomatoes you can also tag type, days-to-maturity, and color — they're kept auto-sorted by maturity in one box, and the 🌐 next to a variety searches for it online. Entries lock 48 hours after you log them. Finished Seed Packets, below Notes, tracks what's run out so you know what to reorder.

The buttons above also cover Full Calendar, Compare Seasons, Rain Log, and Daily Journal; By Month further down breaks the season out month by month. The Harvests tab logs what you actually pick, plus its own Harvest Calendar.

Your data syncs automatically — no manual backup needed. Tap 🌙 Night View above anytime for a dark theme.

Biodynamic Calendar

Fruit, Leaf, Root & Flower days from your printed almanac
Fruit days favor plants grown for their fruit or seed — tomatoes, beans, peppers, squash, and grains. Also the best days to harvest and store fruit.
Leaf days favor leafy greens and foliage — lettuce, cabbage, spinach, herbs grown for leaf.
Root days favor root vegetables — carrots, potatoes, onions, beets — and are generally the best transplanting and root-crop harvest days.
Flower days favor flowering ornamentals and plants grown for bloom, and are also given as good days to cut for cut-flowers.
